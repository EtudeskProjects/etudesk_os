# Etudesk — YC 1-Minute Pitch Script

> **Speaker:** Lamine BARRO | **Duration:** ~60 seconds | **Tone:** Calm confidence, founder who's been in the trenches

---

## THE SCRIPT

**[HOOK — 5 sec]**

Hi, I'm Lamine. I'm from Côte d'Ivoire. I built Etudesk — the AI copilot that connects young Africans to jobs and skills.

**[PROBLEM — 12 sec]**

West Africa has 137 million people. Sixty percent are under 25. Every year, 12 million young people enter the job market — but there's no LinkedIn, no Coursera, nothing that works in French, on a phone, for this population. Youth unemployment tops 50% in countries like Senegal. Not because talent is missing — because the infrastructure is.

**[SOLUTION — 12 sec]**

Etudesk is a mobile app with an AI copilot — in French — that does three things: it helps you find jobs and apply with a generated CV, it teaches you skills with personalized quizzes and lessons, and it helps companies find and rank the right candidates. Think of it as LinkedIn plus Duolingo plus an AI recruiter, built for francophone Africa.

**[TRACTION — 12 sec]**

The product is live. Full AI copilot running on GPT-5 with ten integrated tools, three agents, real-time streaming. I've been building this for nine years. I've advised the Ivorian government on their national AI strategy, worked with the African Development Bank, the World Bank, the UN, and Mastercard Foundation. Forbes named us a Top 10 African EdTech. We won the national excellence award — twice.

**[MARKET — 8 sec]**

African EdTech is a 7 billion dollar market growing to 19 billion by 2034. Francophone Africa alone captures 55% of venture funding outside the Big Four. This market is exploding and nobody owns it yet.

**[ASK — 8 sec]**

We're raising to deploy across 10 cities in 8 West African countries. The product is built. The relationships are built. We just need fuel. We're looking for partners who understand that the biggest talent market in human history deserves a real platform.

**[CLOSE — 3 sec]**

I'm Lamine. This is Etudesk. Let's talk.

---

## KEY PRINCIPLES BEHIND THIS SCRIPT

1. **No jargon.** No "multi-agent architecture," no "vector search," no "SSE streaming." YC partners are technical — they'll ask about the tech if they're interested. The pitch sells the *opportunity*, not the stack.

2. **Specificity over hype.** "137 million people," "twelve million enter the job market," "50% unemployment," "nine years" — real numbers, not adjectives.

3. **Founder-market fit is the star.** Lamine isn't a Silicon Valley founder parachuting into Africa. He's been in this market for a decade, he's advised governments, worked with the AfDB and World Bank. That's the moat — say it plainly.

4. **Product is built = de-risked.** Most YC applicants pitch an idea. Etudesk has a full product with AI agents in production. This is the single strongest signal — lead with it in traction.

5. **The last line matters.** "The biggest talent market in human history deserves a real platform" — this reframes the investment from charity to opportunity. That's what YC wants to hear.

---

## PRACTICE TIPS

- **Record yourself.** Play it back. Cut every word that doesn't earn its place.
- **Don't memorize word-for-word.** Know the structure, the key numbers, and the transitions. Let the rest flow naturally.
- **Pause after the problem.** Let "not because talent is missing — because the infrastructure is" land. Silence is confidence.
- **Eye contact on the close.** "Let's talk" while looking directly at the partner.
- **If you go over 60 seconds**, cut from Market (they can read the numbers) — never cut from Problem or Traction.
